📊 Sales Data Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview

The Sales Data Analysis Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance across products, regions, customers, and sales channels.
The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs). It helps understand revenue trends, product profitability, regional performance, customer contributions, and sales distribution.
The primary objective is to support data-driven business decisions by identifying sales patterns, high-performing products, profitable customers, and opportunities for improvement.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify monthly sales trends and seasonal patterns.
- Evaluate product-wise sales and profitability.
- Compare sales performance across different cities and regions.
- Analyze sales distribution across multiple currencies.
- Identify top-performing customers.
- Understand the contribution of different sales channels.
- Present actionable business insights through an interactive dashboard.

📂 Dashboard Structure

1. Sales Overview

Provides a high-level summary of overall business performance.

Key KPIs:

-Total Sales: $154.57M*
-Total Profit: $6.81M*
-Profit Margin: 4.41%
-Total Quantity Sold: 68K (approximately)
-Total Orders: 7,991

Visualizations:

-Monthly Sales Trend
-Sales by Channel
-City Filter
-Year Filter
-Order Date Filter

Key Insights:

-Total reported sales reached 154.57M.
-Wholesale contributed the highest sales share at 53.67%.
-March recorded the highest monthly sales at 14.40M.
-The overall profit margin was 4.41%.

2. Product Analysis
   
Key Metrics:

-Total Products: 15
-Top-Selling Product: Product 7
-Highest Product Revenue: 25.71M
-Highest Product Profit: 1.14M

Visualizations:

-Product Performance Table
- Products by Profit
-Top 10 Products by Sales
-Product-wise Revenue
-Quantity Sold
-Order Count

Key Insights:

-Product 7 generated the highest revenue of 25.71M.
-Product 7 also recorded the highest profit at approximately 1.14M.
-Product 1 generated the second-highest revenue at approximately 25.49M.
-Product-level analysis helps identify high-performing and low-performing products.

3. Regional Analysis

Evaluates sales performance across different cities and geographical locations.

Key Metrics:

-Total Cities: 45
-Top-Performing City: Christchurch
-Currency with Highest Sales Share: NZD

Visualizations:

-Sales by Latitude and Longitude
-Top 12 Cities by Sales
-Sales by Currency Code
-City-wise Sales Comparison

Key Insights:

-Christchurch recorded the highest city-wise sales performance.
-Hamilton also recorded approximately 11.5M in sales.
-NZD accounted for the largest currency share at 37.62%.
-Geographical analysis helps identify important sales regions.

4. Customer Analysis

Focuses on customer contributions and profitability.

Key Metrics:

-Total Customers: 50
-Top Customer: Medline
-Top Customer Revenue: 4.1M

Visualizations:

-Top 10 Customers by Sales
-Customer Profitability
-Customer-wise Order Performance

Key Insights:

-Medline was the highest-revenue customer, generating approximately 4.1M.
-Pure Group followed with approximately 3.8M.
-Customer profitability analysis helps understand customer contribution to overall business performance.

📈 Key Business Insights

-Sales Performance: The business generated approximately 154.57M in reported sales with a profit margin of 4.41%.
-Sales Channel: Wholesale was the leading sales channel, contributing 53.67% of total reported sales.
-Product Performance: Product 7 was the strongest-performing product in both revenue and profit.
-Regional Performance: Christchurch emerged as the leading city among 45 cities.
-Customer Performance: Medline was the top customer based on revenue contribution.
-Currency Distribution: NZD accounted for the largest share of reported sales by currency.

⚠️ Data & Interpretation Notes

-The dashboard was developed using a sales dataset obtained from an external data source.
-The analysis reflects the records available in the selected dataset.
-The dataset contains multiple currency codes. Therefore, aggregate financial figures should be interpreted carefully unless currency values have been converted into a common reporting currency.
-The findings represent patterns within the available dataset and should not automatically be treated as actual business performance.
